To run ECHO on your iPhone 100% independently as a full-screen native-style app:

  1. Open the Files app on your iPhone and go to your Downloads folder (or tap the download arrow in your browser).
  2. Tap echo.html. If it opens in a quick-look preview, tap the Share icon and choose Open in Safari (or save to iCloud Drive and open via Safari).
  3. Once open in Safari, tap the bottom Share button (square with an upward arrow).
  4. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then tap Add.

Launching ECHO from your Home Screen removes all browser bars and runs completely offline.
